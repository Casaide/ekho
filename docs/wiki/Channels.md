# Channels

The **channels** tab lists the channels you're in, and the rest of the server's channels to join.

- **`#bulgaria`** is the mesh channel. Everyone is in it, and it can't be left.
- **Regional channels** (`#sofia`, `#blagoevgrad`, `#varna` and others) are bridged too. Tap one to join; tap a joined one to leave.
- **Radio channels:** a channel paired with one of your radio's channels shows "radio channel 2 and irc", and its messages go over LoRa when radios are around. One with no radio channel is IRC only.
- **Who's here:** each channel shows how many people are in it now. Open its info for the list, and tap someone to message them.
- **Rename:** open its info and tap **edit** to give it a name of your own.

You can also join and leave by typing `/join #sofia` and `/part`: see [[IRC commands]].
