# People

The **contacts** tab lists everyone ekho knows: radio nodes it has heard, and IRC nicks in your channels. Filter by `lora` or `irc` and sort by when they were last seen.

## Tap a name

Tap the name above anyone's message in a channel to open them: your conversation with them, and their info.

## One person, two paths

The same person often shows up twice: as their radio node, and as their IRC nick (`BLAGOY` and `BLAGOY|mt` are the same person). ekho can't know that by itself, as anyone can type any nick. You can:

1. Open one of them, and under **same person as…** tap the other.
2. They're now one contact, reached on both paths, in one conversation.
3. From then on, the same message from them over both paths within two minutes shows once, labelled `[lora + irc]`.

## Who is this nick?

In a person's info, tap **whois** beside their nick. The server's answer shows underneath: real name, host, account, channels, how long they've been idle. Typing `/whois nick` does the same (see [[IRC commands]]).
